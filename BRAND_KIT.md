# Sketch Arena brand kit

## Brand idea

**Bad drawings. Great nights.** Sketch Arena treats creative panic like a spectator sport: warm, loud, tactile and proudly imperfect. The yellow optimist, mint worrier and red instigator are the recurring cast.

## Core lines

- Draw badly. Guess loudly.
- Draw badly. Become legendary.
- No talent? Perfect.
- 45 seconds. Zero dignity.
- The calm before the chaos.

## Visual rules

- Primary ink: `#171514`
- Paper: `#fffaf0`
- Mustard: `#f2c94c`
- Mint: `#70d6a7`
- Tomato: `#ff5f46`
- Use bold condensed headlines, imperfect ink texture and generous margins.
- Keep the humor self-aware and welcoming. The player is in on the joke.
- Avoid generic neon esports styling, crypto clichés and polished corporate mascots.
